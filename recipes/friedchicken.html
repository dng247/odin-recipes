Fried Chicken

Description: A popular and iconic fast food.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 kg (thighs, wings or breasts)
  • Unsweetened fresh milk: 240 ml
  • Flour: 150 g
  • Cornstarch: 50 g
  • Seasonings: salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder
  • Oil for deep frying.

Steps

  1. Prepare & Marinate: Wash and season the chicken. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Coat: Roll the chicken in flour, dip in beaten eggs, and roll in flour again.
  3. Fry: deep-fry in hot oil for 10 minutes until golden brown.
  4. Serve: Drain the excess oil on paper and enjoy.
